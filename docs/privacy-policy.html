RizCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

RizCut is a video editor for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. Contact: therizwan027@gmail.com

Your videos and photos stay on your phone

All editing and exporting happens on your device. RizCut never uploads your videos, photos or music. Files you pick are opened from your device, and exported videos are saved to your gallery.

Using the app without an account

You can use every editing feature without signing in. Drafts (your project edits) are stored only on your device.

Optional account and cloud backup

If you choose to sign in (with Google or with email and password), we use Google Firebase Authentication to create your account. We store your email address and a user ID.

If you tap Back up drafts, your project edit data (clip timings, text you typed, filter settings, file names and on-device file locations) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. Media files are not uploaded.

This data is used only to let you restore your drafts. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Delete drafts on the device from the Home screen, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Delete your account and all cloud backups in the app: Settings → Account & backup → Delete account.
  • Or email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Advertising

The current version shows no ads. A future version may show ads from Google AdMob when you export a video. If that happens, this policy will be updated first and AdMob may use device identifiers as described in Google's advertising policy.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos are chosen through the system picker; RizCut only sees the files you pick.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) to save exported videos to the gallery.
  • Camera and microphone only while you record yourself in Duet mode or record a voiceover. The recording is saved on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Internet only for sign-in and cloud backup.

Children

RizCut is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules so that only your signed-in account can read or write your backups. Data is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data use changes and change the date above.
